# Design System: Creative

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Playful, character-driven design with expressive typography and bold graphics for landing pages and creative projects.

- **Visual style:** modern, sleek, premium creative
- **Color stance:** primary, secondary, neutral, success, warning, danger
- **Design intent:** Clean creative workspace language using subtle slate backdrops, pure white panels, rounded cards, vibrant blue/violet accents, and soft tactile elements.
